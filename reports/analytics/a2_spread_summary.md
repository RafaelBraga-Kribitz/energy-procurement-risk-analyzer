## Interpretation — you are not in Germany

Across the complete years in the window the Austrian price sat above the German one in 47% of hours on average, and the annual mean spread was widest in 2022 at 26.0 EUR/MWh. Since the 2018 bidding-zone split, cross-border capacity is limited, so when it binds the Austrian zone clears on its own, typically at a premium to Germany. For an Austrian buyer this means that German price reporting, German index products and German benchmarks systematically misstate what an Austrian plant pays: a supplier quote that looks cheap against a German reference can still be expensive for Austria, and a hedge bought on a German product leaves the spread unhedged. Procurement benchmarks and contract indexation for this consumer should therefore reference the Austrian zone, and the spread itself is a risk that needs to be budgeted.
