| data/processed stand-ins → future marts | stand-in shape drift could break DM-050/DM-063 downstream |

| T-04-09 | Tampering | synthetic data determinism | medium | mitigate | Single seeded RNG (module constant) → byte-identical reruns (concurrency/reproducibility edge); DuckDB single-writer; unit test pins determinism |
